The fit plots show larger relative discrepancies between calibrated model prices and market prices at low strikes, approximately 0.10 to 0.15.
Calibration used absolute mean squared error (MSE). For a fixed relative error, a low-premium option contributes less to this objective than a high-premium option. This loss weighting may contribute to the poorer relative fit for low-premium options; it does not by itself identify every source of the discrepancy.
Assessing this region requires both absolute and relative errors, alongside the model and surrogate diagnostics.
